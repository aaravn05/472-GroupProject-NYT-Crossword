Section 01

The Weekly Difficulty Curve

The New York Times crossword gets harder every day of the week. Monday puzzles are the easiest while Friday and Saturday puzzles are the hardest. Over the last 30 years the puzzles at the end of the week always use longer words. Saturday puzzles have the longest words with an average of 5.28 letters each.

Section 02

The Anatomy of Grid Fill

Looking at over 780,000 crossword answers from 1993 to 2021 shows how American crosswords are built. Four-letter words are the most common and shows up over 240,000 times. Five-letter and three-letter words are also very common. Puzzle makers use these short words like glue to connect the longer, special theme words together.
